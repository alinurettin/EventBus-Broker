## 1. Problem Statement & Market Landscape
High-speed in-memory message broker with durable partition logs, topic filtering and consumer groups.
Existing tools in this domain suffer from high operational bloat, cloud subscription fees, and complex setup requirements.
EventBus-Broker fills this gap as a lightweight, zero-dependency, self-hosted solution.

## 2. Technology Stack
Node.js, Stream Processing, PubSub, Docker
